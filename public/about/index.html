Our story

Rooted in simple living

welcome home

Alder Meadow is rooted in the beauty of simple living and the quiet comfort of home.

We believe a kitchen is more than the room where meals get made. It's where mornings begin and evenings wind down, where recipes are passed along and conversations run long. The tools in it should feel like they belong there.

So we design kitchen essentials that bring a sense of intention and ease to everyday rituals. Pieces with earthy tones and softened colors, warm materials and a handmade spirit. Pieces you'll want to leave out on the counter.

The name

Where the alder meets the meadow

Alders grow along streams and at the soft edges of meadows. They're sturdy, unassuming trees that quietly enrich the ground around them, and in early spring they hang with catkins long before the leaves arrive.

That's the kind of presence we want in your kitchen: dependable, unfussy and good for everything around it. The meadow brings the rest: wildflowers, open air and the sense that there's always time to slow down.

What inspires us

Clay, wood and wildflowers

  • Hand-thrown pottery

    The organic forms of a potter's wheel: a rim that isn't perfectly round, a glaze that pools just so. We look for that same softness in every shape we make.

  • Wooden tools

    The warmth of a spoon worn smooth by years of stirring. Natural materials age with you and grow more beautiful the more they're used.

  • The natural world

    Anemone, clover and Queen Anne's lace. The grounding beauty of a meadow informs our colors, our illustrations and the pace we hope you'll keep.

How it feels

Earthy tones, softened colors, a handmade spirit

Sage and slate ground everything we make. Around them, a seasonal palette borrowed from the meadow: honey and sepia, rose whisper and fernwood, the silvered bark of the alder itself.

Each package is wrapped in a woven linen texture and framed with wildflowers drawn by hand, so it feels like a gift even when it's for you.
