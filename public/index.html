Brand story

Alder Meadow is rooted in the beauty of simple living and the quiet comfort of home. Inspired by the organic forms of hand-thrown pottery, the warmth of wooden tools, and the grounding beauty of the natural world, we create kitchen essentials that bring a sense of intention and ease to everyday rituals.

With earthy tones, softened colors, and a handmade spirit, Alder Meadow celebrates a slower, more thoughtful way of living — where the things we use every day feel as natural, timeless, and comforting as home itself.

  • Organic forms

    Shapes borrowed from hand-thrown pottery — soft shoulders, gentle curves, nothing sharp for its own sake.

  • Warm materials

    Wood you want to hold, earthy tones and softened colors that sit quietly on the counter.

  • The natural world

    Wildflowers drawn by hand, linen textures and a meadow's calm in every detail.
