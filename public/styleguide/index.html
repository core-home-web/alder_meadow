Brand story

Alder Meadow is rooted in the beauty of simple living and the quiet comfort of home. Inspired by the organic forms of hand-thrown pottery, the warmth of wooden tools, and the grounding beauty of the natural world, we create kitchen essentials that bring a sense of intention and ease to everyday rituals. With earthy tones, softened colors, and a handmade spirit, Alder Meadow celebrates a slower, more thoughtful way of living where the things we use every day feel as natural, timeless, and comforting as home itself.

Voice

Warm

Speak like a friend in the kitchen — welcoming, never salesy.

Calm

Short, unhurried sentences. Let white space and linen do the work.

Intentional

Talk about rituals and ease, not specs and superlatives.

Natural

Draw on the meadow: wood, clay, wildflowers, linen, home.

Say

“Warm wooden handles for stirring, serving and straining.”

Avoid

“The ULTIMATE must-have utensil set — BUY NOW!”

Balance

Linen & white · 60% Slate · 20% Sage · 15%

Grounds stay quiet: linen and white carry most surfaces. Slate is for type and line art, sage for brand moments. Accents tint product color-ways, never large backgrounds behind body copy.

On the web

Forma DJR Micro and Rollerscript Rough are licensed typefaces. The stacks below load them first when a licensed web-font kit (for example Adobe Fonts) is added to the page, and otherwise fall back to the closest open-source matches — Hanken Grotesk for Forma and Caveat for Rollerscript — which is what this page is currently showing.

  • Nesting sizes

1.5 px slate strokes, round caps, 32 px grid. Labels in Medium uppercase, two lines max.

Use of assets

  1. Badge centered at the top of the card, inside the clearance zone.
  2. Ruled label in Light uppercase names the material or feature.
  3. Product name in large Light uppercase — the loudest line on the pack.
  4. Feature icons in a row of two to three, with short uppercase labels.
  5. Piece count in a sage badge, top-right.
  6. Wildflowers frame the lower corners and bleed off the edges, over linen.
  7. Inset outline in 432 C follows the card silhouette.
